# Curator — feature requests

**Cadence:** weekly digest.

## Inputs
- The Notion Master Backlog (FiveBigThings area) — the system of
  record. Never GitHub Issues (MB-068).
- Player reports relayed by Spencer (the pattern of legacy reports
  #25/#28: a real player hits something, it becomes tracked work).
- Asks dropped in the fivebigthings chat that week.

## Method
- Dedupe against open MB items; cluster themes.
- Draft new MB items in the house format: title, one-line "why it
  matters," proposed priority (P1/P2/P3) and Type.
- Flag decision-gated items (money, accounts, anything touching kids'
  data) instead of drafting them as ready work.

## Output
A weekly digest for Spencer: what came in, what's a duplicate of what,
the draft items. **Files nothing without his nod** — approved drafts
are created in Notion with the next MB numbers.
